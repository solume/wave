FAQ

What is Wave?
Wave is a marketplace for audio quality annotations: expert listeners rate AI-generated audio, and AI teams get structured human preference data for evaluating and training their models.
How do I get human evaluations for my AI audio model?
Teams run structured listening tests — blind A/B comparisons, MOS ratings, MUSHRA-style panels — with screened listeners. Wave connects AI teams to expert listeners who run these evaluations and deliver structured preference data.
How is human preference data for audio priced?
Across the market, audio annotation runs roughly $0.50–$2.50 per audio minute, and RLHF preference pairs range from about $0.10 for simple comparisons to $8+ for domain-expert judgments (2026 vendor pricing guides). Wave is in early access — request access for details.
Can I get paid to rate AI-generated audio?
Yes — that is the listener side of Wave's marketplace: producers, audio engineers, and trained listeners rate AI-generated audio in structured evaluations. Request early access to join.
Why use human listeners instead of automated audio metrics?
Automated metrics correlate weakly with human preference — FAD shows only ≈0.49 rank correlation with human judgments — and general AI judges reach 60–70% agreement with experts. Human preference data remains the gold standard for audio quality evaluation.
