FAQ

What is Wave?
Wave is a marketplace for audio quality annotations: expert listeners rate AI-generated audio, and AI teams get structured human preference data for evaluating and training their models.
Why use human listeners instead of automated audio metrics?
Automated metrics correlate weakly with human preference — FAD shows only ≈0.49 rank correlation with human judgments — and general AI judges reach 60–70% agreement with experts. Human preference data remains the gold standard for audio quality evaluation.
What is human preference data on audio used for?
Evaluating generative audio models and training reward models: judge models trained on pairwise human preferences reach roughly 78% agreement with expert listeners, the highest of any automated approach.
